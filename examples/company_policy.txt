Acme Cloud offers three support plans. Starter support provides email support during business hours. Business support provides 24/7 email and chat support with a four-hour target response time for priority-one incidents. Enterprise support provides 24/7 phone, chat, and email support with a one-hour target response time for priority-one incidents.

Customer data is retained for 30 days after account closure unless a longer period is required by law or an active legal hold. Customers can request export of account data before closure.

Production API rate limits are 600 requests per minute for Business accounts and 2,000 requests per minute for Enterprise accounts. Starter accounts are limited to 120 requests per minute.
